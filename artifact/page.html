ONGA BONGA

Get your family back with music.

Sound on. Every riff is cut from the melody of the song already playing, so the guitar only sounds when you land the notes.

The trouble

Your stove ran away

BLAZE the fire-breathing raptor spent six years chained in the kitchen as the family stove. This morning he snapped the chain and took the family with him. The opening plays out in full, with four mini-games, before you ever see a card.

The valley

Walk it, don't pick it

Every human in it comes out of one figure builder, so the whole valley shares a way of being drawn: flat colour, a single shadow that follows the silhouette, heavy brows, big hair, and fur with a ragged hem. Nothing is drawn as a smooth circle — the circles rasterise in chunky steps like everything else.

The ruins are a wide, mostly empty tile map, and the way out is the gate at the far end — but it stays shut until you have put three beasts down and laid a trail the raptor cannot walk past. Beasts patrol with cones of vision: keep out of them, duck into a bush, roll past, or take the fight. Running burns stamina Bronk does not have.

Campfires restore you. Black fog hides an encounter. The shop is a mammoth in a striped awning and a feathered headdress who turns up where he feels like it and is gone by the time you look again.

The fight

Play the riff yourself

A deck of riffs, three energy a turn. Play a card marked with a note and the camera cuts in: the Rock-Axe lies across the bottom of the screen and rune-stones slide right to left down four strings into a bone strum bar. Strike the string the stone lands on and it rings. SICK timing, sustains to hold.

It starts gently: the first riffs of a run have timing windows two and a half times wider than normal, stones that drift in slowly, half the notes and no vocal line at all. It tightens as you play.

In the gaps the song hands you the vocal. An ember ribbon swoops down from the top: hold the chant and steer your voice along it, and the synth sings whatever pitch you are actually on. Strings carry the damage, the voice carries the crowd.

Duel cards are call and answer. The beast plays a phrase, then you play it back.

Controls

WASD
Walk the valley. Arrow keys work too. SHIFT runs, SPACE rolls, E interacts, M opens the map.
ASDF
The four strings, top to bottom. 1 2 3 4 and the arrows are the same four. Hold for sustains.
SPACE + mouse
Sing. Hold it while the ember ribbon is up and steer your voice with the mouse.
1–9
Play the card in that slot, then click the beast you are aiming at.
R
Encore, once the Hype column is full. SPACE does it too, outside a riff.
E
End your turn.
ESC
Menu, settings, your deck. Hold it to skip the opening.
WALK
Drag the stick in the lower left. ACT interacts, RUN sprints, DODGE rolls.
CARDS
Tap a card once to read it, tap again to play it, then tap the beast you are aiming at.
RIFFS
Tap the string a stone lands on — the whole band across the neck is its pad. Two fingers at once play a chord.
VOICE
When the ember ribbon drops in, hold the left edge of the screen and slide your thumb up and down to follow it.

Runs save as you go, so you can close this and pick the hunt back up later. Timing feel off? Open the menu and nudge the offset, or widen the windows. Everything here is drawn and synthesized in the page itself: 177 sprites written as text and compiled at load, twenty songs written as note patterns and played by a Web Audio synth. No images, no audio files, no libraries.
