ONGA BONGA

A stone age rock saga in three acts.

Sound on. Every riff you play is cut from the song currently playing, so the guitar only sounds when you hit the notes.

The quest

King Rex stole the show

He crashed the festival, carried off Princess Petra and scattered your band across the valley. Climb three acts, beat the boss of each, and you get a bandmate back.

Each turn

Three energy, five cards

Riffs attack, moves block, rallies build the crowd. Dinosaurs telegraph their intent above their heads, so you know whether to swing or brace.

Each riff

Play it, don't click it

Attack cards open a four-lane solo. Hit each note as it reaches the bone bar: PERFECT deals 1.5× damage, GOOD deals 1×, a miss deals nothing.

Landed notes fill the HYPE meter. Fill it and the crowd demands an ENCORE: a free epic solo that hits every enemy once per note.

Controls

DFJK
The four frets, left to right. Arrow keys work too.
1–9
Play the card in that slot. Or click it, then click a dinosaur.
SPACE
Encore, once the Hype meter is full.
E
End your turn.
ESC
Menu, settings and your deck.
FRETS
Tap the four pads along the bottom while a riff plays. Two fingers at once play a chord.
CARDS
Tap a card once to read it, tap again to play it, then tap the dinosaur you are aiming at.
MAP
Drag to scroll, tap a node to travel. Landscape is the way to hold it.

Runs are saved as you go, so you can close this and pick the run back up later. Timing feel off? Open the menu and nudge the offset, or widen the hit windows.
